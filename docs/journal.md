## Workplace skills assessment / 职场能力评估

Below is a compact self-assessment of core workplace skills — scores, strengths, weaknesses, and short guidance.

下面是一份职场核心能力的自评：评分、强项、弱项与简短建议。

### 1. Communication / 沟通能力 — 7.5 / 10

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Clear logic / 逻辑清晰
- ✅ Strong technical expression / 技术表达能力强
- ✅ Good documentation / 文档能力不错
- ✅ Can explain complex issues / 能把复杂问题讲明白

Weaknesses / 弱项

- ❌ Sometimes too direct / 有时过于直接
- ❌ When emotional, can challenge rather than communicate / 情绪上来时容易变成Challenge而不是Communicate
- ❌ Needs better audience-switching / 对不同Audience切换能力有待提高

Example: what engineers understand may not be what management wants to hear.

例如：工程师听得懂的话，Management 不一定愿意听。

### 2. Interpersonal Skills / 人际关系能力 — 6 / 10

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Sincere / 真诚
- ✅ Willing to help / 愿意帮助别人
- ✅ Respectful of excellent people / 对优秀的人尊重

Weaknesses / 弱项

- ❌ Expectations of others can be high / 对别人要求偏高
- ❌ Tends to spot problems rather than tend to feelings / 容易看到问题而不是照顾感受
- ❌ Not deliberately cultivating relationships / 不够刻意经营关系

Note: you already noticed — don’t publicly cue people; this is the start of upgrading interpersonal approach.

注意：你自己最近已经意识到：不要公开Cue别人 — 这是人际关系升级的开始。

### 3. Emotional Intelligence / 情绪智能 — 6 / 10

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Begins self-reflection / 开始反思自己
- ✅ Willing to debrief and apologize / 愿意复盘、能认错

Weaknesses / 弱项

- ❌ Easily angered by perceived unfairness / 容易被不公平激怒
- ❌ Sensitive to signals inside organizations / 对组织里的信号比较敏感
- ❌ Prone to speculation / 容易陷入猜测

Good practice: observe more, assume less. 高EQ的人会："观察更多，假设更少。"

### 4. Leadership / 领导力 — 7 / 10

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Sense of direction / 有方向感
- ✅ Willing to take responsibility / 愿意承担责任
- ✅ Likes to improve systems / 喜欢改善系统
- ✅ Influences peers / 影响周围人

Weaknesses / 弱项

- ❌ Leadership often based on technical skill, not yet by persona/vision / 领导更多靠专业能力，还不是靠人格和愿景

True leadership: people follow you because they trust you, not only because you are right.

真正高级领导力：别人不是因为你对而跟你走，而是因为相信你而跟你走。

### 5. Influence / 影响力 — 5 / 10

This is one of the biggest gaps.

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Professionally credible / 专业可信
- ✅ Willing to push initiatives / 愿意推动事情

Weaknesses / 弱项

- ❌ Persuasion needs work / 说服能力一般
- ❌ Alliance-building weak / 联盟建设较弱
- ❌ Stakeholder management weak / Stakeholder Management较弱
- ❌ Don’t like politics but overthink it / 不喜欢政治但又经常思考政治

Current mode: "I am right, so you should listen."  
Desired mode: "Even without formal authority, you want to help me."  

This is the highest ROI skill to invest in for the next 3 years.

### 6. Execution / 执行能力 — 8 / 10

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Delivery focused / 交付导向
- ✅ Willing to do the work / 愿意干活
- ✅ Pushes to solve problems / 推动问题解决
- ✅ Faces problems, doesn’t avoid them / 遇到问题不躲

Risk / 风险

- ❌ Too many directions at once — risks dispersion (Enterprise AI, Writing, Consulting, Career, Investing, Systems, Build, Journal).  
Focus is the challenge, not execution.

过多方向同时推进，容易分散。问题不是不执行，而是聚焦执行什么。

### 7. Problem Solving / 解决问题能力 — 9 / 10

Core talent.

- ✅ Root cause analysis / 根因分析
- ✅ Fast learning / 快速学习
- ✅ Pattern recognition / 找规律
- ✅ Problem decomposition / 拆问题
- ✅ Structured thinking / 结构化思考

You ask: "What’s the essence?" — this is the hallmark.

### 8. Adaptability / 适应能力 — 8 / 10

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Learns fast / 学习快
- ✅ Open to AI / 对AI非常开放
- ✅ Willing to update beliefs / 愿意改变认知

Example: others debate whether AI will change work; you ask how Enterprise AI becomes career opportunity.

### 9. Business Acumen / 商业思维 — 6.5 / 10

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Thinking about ROI and long-term bets / 开始思考ROI与长期下注
- ✅ Aware of career capital / 对Career Capital有意识

Weaknesses / 弱项

- ❌ Limited commercial experience / 真正商业经验不足
- ❌ Limited customer perspective / 客户视角不足
- ❌ Limited value-capture experience / 价值变现经验有限

This is a 5-year priority.

### 10. Organizational Awareness / 组织认知 — 5.5 / 10

Strengths / 强项

- ✅ Observes power structures / 已经开始观察权力结构
- ✅ Sensitive to org dynamics / 对组织动态敏感

Weaknesses / 弱项

- ❌ Over-interpretation risk / 容易过度解读
- ❌ Needs better alliance/interest mapping / 对联盟和利益关系理解还不够成熟
- ❌ Tends to model orgs as logic systems rather than emotional systems / 容易把组织看成逻辑系统


## Summary / 总结

- Radar highlights: strongest — Problem Solving (9), Execution (8), Adaptability (8), Communication (7.5).  
- Key gaps: Influence (5), Organizational Awareness (5.5).  

Definition: you are a high-potential technical Builder, not yet a Manager.  
To raise your ceiling, convert: Problem Solving (9) × Influence (5) → Problem Solving (9) × Influence (8).

Invest in influence, org cognition, stakeholder management — these are multiplicative.
